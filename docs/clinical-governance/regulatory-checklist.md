# Regulatory and governance checklist (Australia and New Zealand)

This is a checklist of things to assess, **not** a compliance claim. Rules change: confirm the current position with
the relevant regulator or adviser. Status values: _Not started_, _In progress_, _Evidence held_, _Not applicable (with reason)_.

| Area                               | What to assess                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | Evidence needed                                   | Status                         | Owner |
| ---------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ | ----- |
| TGA — software as a medical device | Whether the app (especially the dose calculator and branching logic) is a medical device; whether the clinical decision support exclusion in the _Therapeutic Goods (Excluded Goods) Determination 2018_ (2021 amendments) applies; personal use vs supply | Written intended-use statement; regulatory advice | Not started                    | —     |
| NZ — Medsafe                       | Current medical device rules (legislation in transition); WAND notification if applicable                                                                                                                                                                  | Advice from Medsafe                               | Not started                    | —     |
| Medicines authority                | NT Scheduled Substance Treatment Protocols (or the equivalent in your jurisdiction) — the app must not appear to authorise supply/administration                                                                                                           | Confirm arrangements; disclaimer wording          | In progress (disclaimer)       | —     |
| CARPA licensing                    | Permission to reproduce/adapt/host                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Written permission                                | Not started                    | —     |
| Privacy (AU)                       | Privacy Act 1988 (APPs) and territory/state law (e.g. NT Information Act 2002); Privacy Impact Assessment for organisational use                                                                                                                           | PIA                                               | Not started                    | —     |
| Privacy (NZ)                       | Health Information Privacy Code 2020                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | PIA                                               | Not applicable until NZ use    | —     |
| NSQHS Standards                    | Clinical Governance, Medication Safety, Communicating for Safety (ISBAR), Recognising and Responding to Acute Deterioration — how the app supports them                                                                                                    | Mapping document                                  | Not started                    | —     |
