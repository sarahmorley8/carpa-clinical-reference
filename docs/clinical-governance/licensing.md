# Licensing and permission to reproduce CARPA content

**Owning or having access to the CARPA Standard Treatment Manual does not by itself give permission to reproduce,
adapt or redistribute its content in an app.**

Before entering real content:

1. Identify the copyright holder and current edition (see the imprint page of the manual) and record them in
   `content/references/sources.json`.
2. Ask the publisher, in writing, for permission covering: transcription into structured form, display in this app,
   who may use it (just you / named colleagues / an organisation), and hosting.
3. Check whether an official digital version already meets the need — a parallel copy carries a risk of falling out of
   date.
4. Keep the permission with the governance records. Note any conditions (attribution, access restriction, expiry).

Hosting matters: a site on a public URL is distribution, even if the link is not advertised. If permission requires
restricted access, use a host that supports it (e.g. Cloudflare Pages with Cloudflare Access).

Until permission is confirmed the app contains placeholders and synthetic demonstration content only.
