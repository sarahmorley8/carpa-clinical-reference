# Visual design

## Palette

Warm earth colours drawn from Country: sand backgrounds, river teal (primary actions), eucalyptus sage, ochre
(caution), peach, and terracotta red (critical/escalate). Clinical signals are unchanged in meaning: red = critical,
amber/ochre = caution, every colour also carries text and an icon. Contrast is checked automatically (WCAG 2.2 AA,
light and dark).

## Aboriginal artwork — what this app does and does not do

The app does **not** imitate Aboriginal art styles, dot painting or symbols. Many motifs (for example concentric
circles, U shapes, track and travel lines) carry cultural meaning and belong to particular peoples and artists.
Reproducing or imitating them without permission is a recognised form of cultural appropriation and can breach
Indigenous Cultural and Intellectual Property (ICIP) protocols.

The decorative band on the Home screen (`src/components/LandscapeBand.tsx`) is a generic rolling-hills placeholder in
the earth palette.

### To use genuine Aboriginal artwork

1. Commission or license a work from an Aboriginal artist, ideally through an Aboriginal-owned art centre, ideally from
   the communities the app will serve.
2. Agree in writing: permitted use (app header, icon, splash), crop/recolour permissions, attribution wording, payment
   and duration, and any cultural restrictions (e.g. on use near sickness or death content).
3. Replace the band with the artwork image (in `public/artwork/`) and add the artist credit and story (as the artist
   wishes it told) to Settings.
4. For organisational rollout, include this in the cultural safety consultation (see the regulatory checklist).
